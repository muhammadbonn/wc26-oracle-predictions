### 1. Base Match Outcome Prediction
* ***Correct Outcome (Win/Draw/Loss):*** You earn ***+3 points***.
* ***Incorrect Outcome:*** You earn ***0 points***.

---

### 2. Advanced Score Prediction (Optional)
If you activate Advanced Score Prediction, the following applies:
* ***Home/Away Goals Correct:*** You earn ***+2 points*** for each correctly predicted goal count.
* ***Home/Away Goals Incorrect:*** You earn ***-1 point*** for each incorrect goal count.
* ***Perfect Score Bonus:*** Predicting both home and away goals perfectly grants a total of ***+5 points*** (instead of 4).

---

### 3. Knockout & Penalty Shootout Rules (The "Pro" Rules)
* ***Standard Qualification:*** If you correctly predict the winning team in a knockout match, you earn ***+3 points***.
* ***Penalty Shootout Bonus:*** If you correctly predict that the match will go to penalties, you receive ***+2 bonus points*** (Total = 5 points).
* ***Penalty Penalty:*** If you predict a penalty shootout but the match ends in regular time, you receive ***-1 point***.
* ***Smart Prediction:*** If you predict the wrong winner, but correctly predict that the match will go to penalties, you receive ***+1 point*** as a "consolation" for reading the match tension correctly.
* ***Master of Penalties:*** If you predict the specific penalty score (goals for each team in the shootout) and get it ***correct***, you earn ***+3 points*** for each team. If ***incorrect***, you receive ***-1 point*** for each wrong guess.
